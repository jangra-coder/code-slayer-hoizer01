### 1 · Hook (0:00–0:21) · Title slide
Think about the last time your phone or laptop stopped working. You took it to a shop, someone quoted a number, and you had to decide, right there, whether to repair it or buy a new one. Most of us make that call blind. We're **[Team Name]**, and we built RepairOS to fix that.

### 2 · Problem (0:21–0:50) · Slide 2
In a 2025 Counterpoint survey, 43 percent of Indian smartphone users said repairs were expensive, and 4 in 10 had to visit the service centre more than once for a single issue. India generated 1.75 million tonnes of e-waste last year, up 72 percent in five years. *(optional)* And this surprised us: about 72 percent of a phone's climate impact happens before you even switch it on. So the fault isn't really the problem. **The decision is.**

### 3 · Solution (0:50–1:35) · Slide 3
RepairOS sits between a broken device and a new purchase. In one five-minute session, it does five things.
It **identifies** your device from a photo of its model label.
It **collects real evidence**: not just what you type, but data from the device itself, like the Windows battery report or browser tests of the screen, speaker and camera.
It **ranks the likely faults** with a confidence score, and asks the one question that narrows it down fastest.
Then it gives a **decision in rupees**: repair, inspect, or replace, with a fair price range to check any shop's quote.
And it saves everything in a **Repair Passport** that stays with the device when you sell it.

### 4 · Technology (1:35–1:54) · Slide 4
One deliberate choice: **the AI never makes the final call.** A language model reads messy input, even Hinglish, and turns it into clean symptoms. A Bayesian engine and clear rules decide, and a safety check stops anything risky, like a swollen battery, and sends you to a professional.

### 5 · Feasibility and business (1:54–2:12) · Slides 5 and 6
For the hackathon, we've kept it tight: phones and laptops, ten common faults, one complete working flow. Diagnosis is free. We earn from verified reports, repair-shop bookings and trade-in referrals, so we earn whether a device is repaired, resold or recycled. **That keeps our advice honest.**

### 6 · Close (2:12–2:15) · Back to the title slide
RepairOS. **Diagnose before you replace.** Thank you.

Hand over without saying "now X will explain". The next person just starts their line.

## Making it sound like yours
- **Use a real story if you have one.** If someone on the team has actually had a dead battery or been given an inflated repair quote, open with that instead of "Think about the last time…". One true sentence beats any script.
- **Don't read it word for word.** Learn the six beats and the bold lines, and say the rest in your own words. Judges can hear reading.
- **Know one level deeper than you say.** Be ready to explain why the AI doesn't decide (an LLM can sound confident while it is wrong, but a rule can be checked) and where the confidence score comes from (Bayesian probabilities from known failure rates for each model).
- **Say the numbers slowly**, then pause after "The decision is." and after "That keeps our advice honest."

## Recording tips
- Show the slides full screen with a small face-cam in a corner. Seeing a face builds trust.
- Use a wired earphone mic or a phone held 20 cm away, in a quiet room with soft furnishings.
- Record each part separately, then join them. It's easier to fix one part than redo the whole video.
- Export at 1080p and check that the final length is between 2:00 and 2:30 before uploading.
